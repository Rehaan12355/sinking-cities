Why you can trust the numbers

Checked against GPS

In Hampton Roads, radar and GPS sinking rates agree to about 1.4 mm a year at the nine stations inside the map.

Ranges, not single years

Every flood date runs from a high sea level scenario with faster sinking to a low one with slower sinking.

Open about limits

It compares ground height with tide height. It does not model storms, rain, drains or sea walls, and the site says so.

Every number traces to public data: NASA OPERA and NISAR radar, NASA sea level projections, USGS elevation, NOAA tide gauges and the US Census.

OPERA DISP-S1 is produced by NASA from the European Space Agency’s Copernicus Sentinel-1 radar.
